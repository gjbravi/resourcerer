resourcerer is managing VPAs with selection={{ .Values.selection }} and defaultMode={{ .Values.defaultMode }}.

{{- if eq .Values.selection "label" }}

Opt workloads in by labeling them or their namespace:
  kubectl label namespace <ns> resourcerer.io/enabled=true
{{- else }}

Every workload outside these namespaces gets a VPA:
  {{ include "resourcerer.excludedNamespaces" . }}
Opt out with:
  kubectl label namespace <ns> resourcerer.io/enabled=false
{{- end }}

Requires the Vertical Pod Autoscaler (recommender, and updater + admission controller for Auto mode).

Open the UI:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "resourcerer.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  open http://localhost:8080
