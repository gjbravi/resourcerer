## Why resourcerer

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Runs in your cluster" icon="server">
    One Helm chart, three small Deployments. Usage history comes from the Prometheus you already run; state lives in a
    `Recommendation` custom resource. No SaaS, no agent, no database.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Explainable numbers" icon="magnifier">
    Every value is a documented percentile of usage plus a margin, such as "p95 + 10% over 7d". There is no model to
    trust blindly: you can check each number against the charts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A verdict, not just data" icon="approve-check">
    Under the apply button, the workload page says whether to apply: "Safe to apply", "Review before applying" or
    "Apply to fix reliability", from history coverage, a replay of usage and reliability signals.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Costs that match your nodes" icon="star">
    Monthly costs from configured rates, or calibrated to Karpenter's price estimates, and optionally priced at the
    nodes each workload actually ran on, spot or on-demand.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Applies on your terms" icon="setting">
    Apply and revert from the UI, copy a Kustomize or Helm patch into Git, or turn on auto-deploy. Apply policies
    decide when an apply may start: windows, freezes and rate limits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GitOps friendly" icon="random">
    Settings are annotations, policies are custom resources, applies use server-side apply with their own field
    manager, so Argo CD and Flux can tell them apart.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
